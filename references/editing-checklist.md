# Pre-publish editing checklist

Complete this pass after drafting and after substantive edits. Review the full [style guide](style-guide.md) alongside these checks.

## Meaning and evidence

- [ ] The piece has a clear central idea. Each paragraph advances it or supplies necessary evidence.
- [ ] Each material technical claim has a supporting source checked before drafting.
- [ ] A local knowledge base was queried first if available. Relevant primary sources were checked for current scope.
- [ ] Product names, versions, dates and conditions agree with the sources.
- [ ] Statistics retain their units, scope and provenance. Calculations have been checked.
- [ ] Facts, firsthand reports, opinions and inferences are distinguishable where it affects interpretation.
- [ ] Every personal experience, quotation and number is supplied or sourced. None was invented for the hook.
- [ ] Material limitations remain beside the claims they qualify. Uncertainty is precise and free of stacked hedges.
- [ ] Conflicting or unsupported claims have been resolved, removed or narrowed. Any remaining verification issue is reported outside the copy.

## Voice and structure

- [ ] The opening offers a specific observation, tension or supported claim.
- [ ] The vocabulary, rhythm and stance match the supplied voice samples or the agreed provisional register.
- [ ] The copy explains the technical mechanism at the audience's level.
- [ ] Opinions are supported and consistent with the author's expressed position.
- [ ] Each point appears once. Paragraph endings add information rather than restating the paragraph.
- [ ] Sentence and paragraph lengths vary naturally. Repeated openings and consecutive tricolons are gone.
- [ ] Lists, headings and section lengths follow the substance and requested format.
- [ ] The ending lands a consequence or a specific question. Generic engagement requests and recap padding are gone.

## Punctuation and formatting

- [ ] Search for Unicode U+2014. There are zero em dashes in authored prose.
- [ ] Search for `**`, `__`, `<b>` and `<strong>`. Remove decorative emphasis and bold-led bullet habits. Keep bold only when a recurring structure needs labels.
- [ ] Semicolons and Oxford commas are absent unless explicitly requested.
- [ ] Unicode U+2026 ellipses are replaced with three periods or the sentence is rewritten.
- [ ] Decorative emoji, special-character flourishes and Markdown artifacts are gone.
- [ ] Exact code, commands and required quotations remain accurate after prose edits.

## Self-commentary and banned patterns

- [ ] Review every list in style-guide sections 1 and 2. Remove banned words, filler and stock transitions.
- [ ] Remove negation pivots, questions answered immediately and empty ranges from style-guide section 4.
- [ ] Search for `this article`, `this piece`, `above`, `below`, `let me` and `I'm being`. Cut prose whose only subject is the prose. Review matches in context.
- [ ] Search for `sit with`, `notice` and `consider for a second`. Remove reader instructions that delay the point. Retain concrete task instructions needed for a technical walkthrough.
- [ ] Qualifications state the limitation itself. At most one honesty signpost remains, only if it states a substantive stance.
- [ ] Provenance and substantive authorial asides still convey useful information. Performance of rigor and announcements of concessions are gone.

## Links and delivery

- [ ] Open every link supplied with the deliverable, including any first-comment links. Confirm the destination exists and supports the associated claim.
- [ ] Check the linked page's date, version and scope. A successful page load alone doesn't verify the claim.
- [ ] A login screen, inaccessible page or search snippet isn't recorded as a checked source. Report blocked access outside the copy.
- [ ] Blog and article citations sit near the claims they support. LinkedIn citation placement follows the brief, with first-comment source links as the default.
- [ ] LinkedIn posts stand alone. Hashtags are optional, limited to three and placed at the end.
- [ ] The copy contains no placeholders, research notes or embedded revision instructions.
- [ ] Read the final copy for cadence and reread every technical statement changed during editing.
- [ ] Describe the draft as ready to publish only when no factual verification remains unresolved.
